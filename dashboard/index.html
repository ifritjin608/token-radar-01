How a row is scored

Implied value = TVL × 0.25 × growth × maturity × breadth. Growth is the 7-day TVL change, clamped to −60…+120% and weighted 0.6. Maturity rises with the days a protocol has run with no token, from 0.6 at listing to 1.0 after three years. Breadth adds 5% per extra chain, up to four. Nothing is hardcoded by name: the same four numbers score every row.

The board covers the whole watchlist — every protocol at or above $50M TVL that records no token and is not a wrapped, staked or bridged product — plus every other rankable pre-token protocol below the floor. “Records no token” is what the snapshot says; it is not proof a token does not exist.
